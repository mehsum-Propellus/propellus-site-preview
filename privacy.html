Who we are

Propellus Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202307570R), 1 North Bridge Road, #03-23, High Street Centre, Singapore 179094 ("Propellus", "we", "us"). We provide visa processing software to travel agencies, online travel agencies and other business customers ("agencies").

Whose data is it

  • Our own data. We are responsible for data about website visitors, people who fill in our forms, our customers' account users, and business contacts.
  • Applicant data. When an agency uses Propellus to process a traveller's visa application, that data belongs to the agency. We process it only on the agency's instructions and under our agreement with them. If you are a traveller, please contact your agency first, and we will help them deal with your request.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, work email, company and phone number.
  • Account and usage data for people who use our platform.
  • Website usage data collected through cookies (see "Cookies" below).
  • For agencies, applicant data: passport and travel document details and scans, identity and contact details, supporting documents, and application status.

Why we use it

  • To provide, run and support our services.
  • To process visa applications for agencies.
  • To answer enquiries and demo requests.
  • To send service updates and, if you have agreed to them, product news.
  • To improve our website and services, and to keep them secure.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use applicant data for advertising.

Consent

By giving us your data, you agree that we can use it as described here. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing frank@propellus.co. If you do, we will tell you what it means for the services you receive.

Who we share it with

  • The visa authority or official visa channel handling the application.
  • The agency acting for the traveller.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract, including cloud hosting providers, Google (Google Analytics 4) and HubSpot (forms and customer records).

Transfers outside Singapore

When data leaves Singapore, we use contracts and other safeguards to make sure it is protected to a standard comparable to Singapore law.

How we protect it

We encrypt data in transit, limit access to staff who need it, and log access to sensitive data. Our Security page has more detail.

Data breaches

If a breach is likely to cause significant harm, we will tell the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) and the affected customers and individuals, as the law requires, within 3 days of confirming it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you and to have it corrected. We will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also be able to ask us to delete your data or to stop using it.

Data Protection Officer

Francisco Humarang (CTO), frank@propellus.co. If you are not happy with our reply, you can contact the PDPC at www.pdpc.gov.sg.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. This policy is governed by Singapore law.

Cookies

We use cookies that are needed for the website to work. If you agree, we also use:

  • Google Analytics 4 (cookies such as _ga and _ga_*) to see how visitors use the site.
  • HubSpot (cookies such as __hstc, hubspotutk, __hssc and __hssrc) to run our forms and recognise returning visitors.

You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer or in your browser settings.
