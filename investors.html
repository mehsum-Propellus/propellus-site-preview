WHY THIS CANNOT BE COPIED

The answer every investor asks.

You can copy the code. You cannot copy the history.

A competitor starting today has no application history and no corridor intelligence. That history only comes through OTA integrations, and those take years to build.

01 / Distribution — first-mover

Distribution through OTAs.

Our CCO was Co-CEO at TX Group and CDO at Companjon — activating the commercial channel for visa-required travel.

02 / Global network

Warm doors. First-name basis.

Travel Growth Partners — 20 years across Rakuten and Mystifly. Warm OTA contacts at VP level and above. These doors open on a first-name basis. Cannot be replicated from cold outreach.

03 / Aligned incentives — the moat an incumbent cannot copy

The outsourced model earns from optional services sold at the point of applicant anxiety. Propellus earns when applications are processed correctly — GDPR-compliant by architecture. An incumbent cannot copy this without dismantling its own business model.

Our investors know this problem first-hand.
