Fully configurable visa workflows

New destinations.
The same connected platform.

Your visa workflow starts with three things: where the traveller is going, the passport they hold and where they live. Propellus connects those settings to the requirements, documents and guided application journey.

01

Destination

Define the visa offering and the requirements for the trip.

02

Nationality

Apply the eligibility rules and document requirements for the traveller’s passport.

03

Residency

Adapt the questions and requirements to where the traveller lives.

Requirements that follow the traveller.

Document requirements and checks can vary by visa, nationality, residency and applicant type. The guided conversation asks for the information that applies to that journey.

AI within a configured workflow.

Extraction turns documents into structured information. Configured checks flag mismatches, and the application workspace brings the answers and documents together for human review.

Expand through configuration.

The Propellus team configures the visa offering, document requirements and application journey for a new market. Supported Smart Bridge portal handoffs are confirmed separately during setup.

Bring us the markets you serve. We’ll map the configuration and confirm the application and portal workflow with your team.

The work shouldn’t stop
at a prepared file.

Take reviewed application details into a supported government visa portal, without starting the data entry again.

  2. Smart Bridge opens the supported portal. Your team completes sign-in and authentication.
  3. Run Smart Bridge to populate supported fields and attach documents. Your team reviews the portal application and submits.
