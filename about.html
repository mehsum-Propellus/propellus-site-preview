HOW IT STARTED

A single digit. A missed flight. A company.

Our CEO Mehsum Sayani spent 22 years at Standard Chartered building the rails of consumer finance — branch banking, credit cards, payments, identity, and a $150M cybersecurity transformation at bank scale.

Then, on a family trip to London, his son was denied boarding at the Dubai gate. One visa digit was wrong. The trip collapsed at the airline counter.

He recognised it as a fixable rails problem, and AI made now the moment.
