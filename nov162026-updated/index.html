Before we talk

Questions agencies ask

Does Propellus submit visa applications?
No. Propellus prepares and checks the application. Your team reviews it and submits.
Which destinations, nationalities and residencies can you support?
Propellus is configurable by destination, nationality and residency. Our team maps the eligibility rules, documents and application journey for the markets you serve. We confirm the active visa workflow and any supported Smart Bridge portal handoff before rollout.
Who is Propellus for?
Travel agencies, OTAs and visa fulfilment partners. Propellus is B2B only and never sells to travellers.
What does Propellus actually do?
It reads passports and documents, checks them against destination rules and flags gaps, then prepares the application for the official visa site, ready for your team to review.
What results do teams see?
GoZayaan’s visa operations team reported less time spent on repetitive manual work. Read their customer story for the context; results depend on the workflow and the team using it.
How is passport data protected?
Data is encrypted in transit and used for visa processing only. It is never sold and never used for advertising. See our Security page.

Book a demo. We’ll map your destinations, volumes and workflow, and show an application using sample documents.
