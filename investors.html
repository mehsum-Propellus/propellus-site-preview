Thesis

Rails. Not tools.

Card payment networks don't issue cards. Travel booking systems don't sell flights. Propellus doesn't issue visas. The pattern works because the rails compound, and only one operator gets to own them.

OperatorIssues the productRuns the railsEarns per event
Card payment networkNoYesPer transaction
Travel booking systemNoYesPer booking
PropellusNoYesPer application

The OTA already uses a booking system to book the flight. The same OTA needs Propellus to prepare the visa application. One completes the booking. Propellus completes the compliance. The traveller cannot fly until both layers have done their job.

Why this cannot be copied

You can copy the code. You cannot copy the history.

A competitor starting today has no application history and no destination intelligence. That history only comes through OTA integrations, and those take years to build.

Aligned incentives

The moat an incumbent cannot copy

The outsourced model earns from optional services sold at the point of applicant anxiety. Propellus earns when applications are prepared correctly, GDPR-compliant by architecture. An incumbent cannot copy this without dismantling its own business model.
