How it started

One wrong digit.
A journey that never started.

On a family trip to London, one digit on Mehsum’s son’s visa was wrong. The trip ended before it began.

After 22 years at Standard Chartered, working across banking, cards, payments and identity, Mehsum recognised a preparation problem that better infrastructure could help address.

He had led a $150M cybersecurity transformation at bank scale. Propellus brings that experience to a different kind of journey: helping travel partners prepare applications while keeping people in control.
