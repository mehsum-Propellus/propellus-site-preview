The complete application journeyInside the product

Start with a conversation.

The assistant asks for the next detail, offers suggested answers and keeps the application summary alongside the conversation.

Upload once. Reduce the typing.

Travellers provide their documents inside the same flow. Document extraction turns the uploaded information into application details.

Check the details. Prepare the form.

Extracted information appears in structured fields. Review it against the documents, resolve missing or inconsistent details, and continue.

A correction, explainedIf the itinerary and passport spell a name differently, request the corrected document and review it before the handoff.

Keep people in control.

The agency workspace keeps applications, status and ownership together. Your team resolves outstanding issues and reviews the file before the portal handoff.

Smart Bridge carries the work across.

Open the supported government portal and sign in. Run Smart Bridge to transfer the reviewed information and documents into the application, reducing the need to enter them again.
