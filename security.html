WHAT WE COLLECT AND WHY

What we do — and don't do — with your data.

COLLECT

Passport and application data

Passport data, travel document scans, and application information submitted by agents and travelers for visa processing.

USE

For visa processing — only

Solely to process applications on behalf of agents and travelers. No data sold to third parties. No advertising profiling.

RETAIN

Retention with control

Kept only as long as needed to process and meet legal obligations, then deleted or archived per your data retention policy.

Role-based access

Agent data is visible only to that agency's account. Propellus operations staff access is logged and audited.

Detailed retention schedules are defined per engagement in our data processing agreements.
