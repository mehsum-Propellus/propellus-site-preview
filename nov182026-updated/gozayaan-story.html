Inside the product

Document information becomes structured application data for review.

The challenge: repeated work around every file.

Visa operations involve more than collecting a passport. The team has to gather the right information, check documents, prepare application data and follow up when something needs attention.

GoZayaan’s feedback described repetitive manual work across those stages, including collection, checking, data entry and follow-ups.

The workflow: bring preparation together.

Propellus supports a structured preparation workflow, helping the team work with traveller information and documents in the context of an application.

  • Gather the information needed to prepare the file.
  • Read documents and make details available for checking.
  • Identify items that need a recheck or follow-up.
  • Keep the operations team responsible for review and next steps.

The experience: useful progress, with refinements continuing.

Shafkat reported less repetitive work and a more structured process for the visa operations team. The feedback also highlighted continued improvements and the need for people to review the application.

This story reflects GoZayaan’s visa-operations feedback from September 2026. Features and integration scope depend on the agreed workflow.

What this means for another OTA.

The useful question is where your team spends time rebuilding a file or repeating the same checks. In a demo, we can map those steps, show the relevant product flow and establish what fits your operation.
