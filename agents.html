HOW IT WORKS

Four steps.

01 / Intake
Customer or agent uploads the passport. Propellus OCR extracts all traveler identity data automatically.
02 / AI validates
AI checks the data against destination visa requirements and flags errors before your team submits.
03 / Form auto-filled
The application is filled in from the extracted data. Your team reviews and confirms.
04 / Your team reviews and submits
Your team checks the prepared application and submits it.
