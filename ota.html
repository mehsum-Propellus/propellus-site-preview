THE GAP

Today the visa sits outside the basket.

Flights, hotel and insurance go in the basket. The visa doesn't, so the traveller leaves your app to work out the requirements and the paperwork alone.

THE VISA STEP

How the visa step works.

  1. 1

    Add the visa

    The traveller adds the visa to the trip, next to flights, hotel and insurance.

  2. 2

    Read the passport

    Propellus reads the passport and shows each field with a confidence check.

  3. 3

    Flag any gaps

    A missing or unclear document is flagged, with a suggested fix.

  4. 4

    Ready for your team

    The application is prepared, checked and marked ready for your team to submit.
