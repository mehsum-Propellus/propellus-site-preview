How it works

Four steps. Your team has the final say.

Every application is approved by a person on your team before it's submitted.

  1. Upload

    Your traveller or agent uploads the passport and documents.

  2. Check

    The E-Visa AI Assistant reads them and runs 50+ automated checks against destination rules. Gaps are flagged before anything is filed.

  3. Prepare

    The form is prepared, documents sorted, resized and attached. Smart Bridge fills supported visa portals.

  4. Your team reviews and submits

    Your team has the final say. Track every application in one place.
