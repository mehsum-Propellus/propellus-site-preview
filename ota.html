THE GAP

Your customers book the trip. The visa is left to them.

Visa requirements are fragmented, so most booking flows stop at the ticket. Propellus adds the visa step to the trip your customer is already booking.

FOR THE TRAVELLER

Your traveller is guided, step by step.

Inside your app, the traveller sees exactly what to bring: passport, photo, bank statements, bookings. Each document has its own step and a clear status, so nothing is missed on the way in.

MISSING DOCUMENTS

Questions and missing documents, handled in the flow.

No more back-and-forth for one application. When a passport is expired, a photo is too small or a statement is missing, the traveller gets a clear request and a suggested fix in chat, and can reply or re-upload right there.

HOW IT PREPARES

Read. Cross-check. Flag gaps. Prepare.

Propellus AI reads every document, cross-checks and validates the details, highlights potential gaps and helps fix them. Then it prepares the visa form and sorts, resizes and attaches every document.
